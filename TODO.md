# Snap Call: launch to-do

What's left before Snap Call goes on the App Store. Tick a box when it's done.

## Blockers (can't launch without these)

- [ ] **Join the Apple Developer Program ($99/year).** Needed for the App Store and TestFlight. Only you can sign up; once you have, the app can be on TestFlight the same day.
- [ ] **App Store pages and info:**
  - [ ] Privacy policy page (no accounts, no data collected, rooms deleted after the game)
  - [ ] Support page
  - [ ] Screenshots at the sizes Apple asks for
  - [ ] Age rating: 4+, not the Kids category (its rules on links clash with the room share link)
  - [ ] Short description
- [ ] **Keep Play a friend inside Cloudflare's free plan.** Today each open game keeps its room awake the whole time, which could use up the free limit if many people play at once. Switch to connections that let rooms sleep between moves (about half a day), or pay $5/month.
