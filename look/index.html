    Play by play

      How to play

      • Get past the Red Zone for a touchdown (7 points), or kick a field goal there (3 points).
      • The offense picks a play, the defense guesses it with a Stop card, and both flip at once.
      • Not stopped (wrong guess): Run and Short Pass gain 1 zone, Deep Pass gains 2.
      • Stopped (right guess): the defense takes over at its 25, or midfield on a Deep Pass.
      • First to 21 wins, or pick 28 or 35 at New game.

      Specials

      Play one with your card, before the flip. A Save works only if you lose the snap, a Bonus only if you win it. Only one works per snap. Once played, it’s gone, even if it doesn’t work. If their Challenge Flag cancels the snap, you get yours back.

      You start with 2, draw 1 when you’re scored on, and hold up to 3. The marks by CPU show how many it has.

      Juke or Spectacular Catch offense save
      If your play is stopped, you still gain 1 zone, even on a Deep Pass.
      Sack or Tackle for Loss defense save
      If you don’t stop the play, they go back 1 zone instead of gaining (never behind their own 25).
      Challenge Flag either side, save
      If you lose the snap, cancel it and both sides pick again.
      Breakaway offense bonus
      If your Run isn’t stopped, it’s a touchdown.
      Hail Mary offense bonus
      If your Deep Pass isn’t stopped, it’s a touchdown.
      Pick Six defense bonus
      If you stop a pass (Stop Short or Stop Deep), you score 7.
      Forced Fumble defense bonus
      If you stop a Run, you get the ball at midfield.

      The computer

      It learns your habits, so mix it up. On Hard it also catches tricks and remembers your last few games.

      Today’s rules: one right guess and the defense gets the ball.
