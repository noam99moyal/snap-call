    How to play

    • Get past the Red Zone for a touchdown (7 points), or kick a field goal there (3 points).
    • The offense picks a play, the defense tries to guess it with a Stop card, and both flip at once.
    • Not stopped (wrong guess): Run and Short Pass gain 1 zone, Deep Pass gains 2.
    • Stopped (right guess): the defense takes over at its 25, or midfield on a Deep Pass.
    • Stopped once (right guess): the offense tries again in the same zone.
    • Stopped twice in the same zone: the defense takes over at its 20, or midfield on a Deep Pass.
    • First to 21 wins, or pick 28 or 35 at New game.

    Specials

    Each player can play one special with their play card, before the flip. Saves work when a snap is lost, Bonuses when it’s won, and a Save always beats a Bonus. A played special is gone either way, unless the other side’s Challenge Flag cancels the snap or the offense kicks a field goal: then it goes back to its owner.

    Each player starts with 2 (3 for whoever gets the ball second), draws 1 when scored on, and holds up to 3. The marks by CPU show how many it has.

    Juke or Spectacular Catch offense save
    If the offense is stopped, it still gains 1 zone, even on a Deep Pass.
    Sack or Tackle for Loss defense save
    If the defense doesn’t stop the play, the offense goes back 1 zone (never behind its own 25).
    If the defense doesn’t stop the play, the offense goes back 1 zone (never behind its own 20).
    Challenge Flag either side, save
    The side that loses the snap cancels it, and both sides pick again.
    Breakaway offense bonus
    If the Run isn’t stopped, it’s a touchdown.
    Hail Mary offense bonus
    If the Deep Pass isn’t stopped, it’s a touchdown.
    Pick Six defense bonus
    If the defense stops a pass (Stop Short or Stop Deep), it scores 7.
    If the defense stops a pass (Stop Short or Stop Deep), even on the offense’s first try, it scores 7.
    Forced Fumble defense bonus
    If the defense stops a Run, it gets the ball at midfield.
    If the defense stops a Run, even on the offense’s first try, it gets the ball at midfield.

    The computer

    It learns your habits, so mix it up. On Hard it also catches tricks and remembers your last few games.
