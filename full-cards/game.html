    How to play

    • The field has 3 zones between you and the end zone: Own 25, Midfield, Red Zone.
    • Every snap, the offense secretly picks a play and the defense secretly guesses it. Both cards are shown at once.
    • Wrong guess: the ball moves. Run and Short Pass move 1 zone. Deep Pass moves 2.
    • Right guess: the defense takes the ball at its own 25. A guessed Deep Pass is an interception, so they start at midfield instead.
    • Touchdown is 7 points. From the Red Zone you can kick a field goal for 3 instead of snapping.
    • After a score, the team that got scored on gets the ball and draws a special card. You can hold 3.
    • First to 21 wins.
    • Press and hold any card to see it big.

    Specials: losers save, winners score

    Every special is a Save or a Bonus. You play it with your card, before the cards are shown. A Save only works if you lose the snap, with any play. A Bonus only works if you win it. Only one special can work per snap, and a played special is used up either way.

    Juke or Spectacular Catch offense save
    If they guess your play, you still gain 1 zone. A guessed Deep Pass gains 1 instead of being intercepted.
    Sack or Tackle for Loss defense save
    If you guess wrong, they gain nothing and go back 1 zone (never behind their own 25). They keep the ball.
    Challenge Flag either side, save
    If you lose the snap, cancel it and both sides pick again. Any other special played on it goes back to its owner.
    Breakaway offense bonus
    If you run and they guess wrong, it goes all the way for a touchdown.
    Hail Mary offense bonus
    If you throw deep and they guess wrong, it goes all the way for a touchdown.
    Pick Six defense bonus
    If you guess their pass right (Stop Short or Stop Deep), you score 7.
    Forced Fumble defense bonus
    If you guess their run right, you recover the ball at midfield.

    The computer

    It remembers which plays you call and which ones you guess, and leans on your habits. Mix it up.

    On Hard it also notices when you try to trick it, and remembers your last few games on this device.
