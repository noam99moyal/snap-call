SNAP CALL

One screen per snap on phones

Today’s game is about 1,500px tall on a phone that shows 664px, so you scroll down to pick a card and up to see what happened. These three mockups fit a whole snap on one screen. They play a cut-down Stopped once game: tap a card, snap, hold any card to see it big. Specials can be picked but don’t do anything here.

Open this page on your phone to try them for real: noam99moyal.github.io/snap-call/mockups/phone/
